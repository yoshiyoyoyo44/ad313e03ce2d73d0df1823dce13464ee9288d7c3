                  THE AUSTRALIA-WEIZMANN INSTITUTE

                               EXCHANGE SCHEME
          The Australian Association for the Weizmann Institute of Science has been
established to promote the exchange of information and exchange visits between
scientists at the Weizmann Institute of Science, Rehovot, Israel, and their colleagues
in Australia . The president of the Association is Professor Sir Gustav Nossal, and
Professor Sir MacFarlane Burnet is Patron . The Weizmann Institute is concerned with
fundamental and applied research in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biophysics-Bio-
chemistry, and Biology . The exchange programme will initially involve short-term
visits of a few weeks duration . In time it is hoped that this can be extended to
cover periods of study leave and postdoctoral study . In addition to such "official"
exchange visits, it is hoped that the Society will be able to arrange for Australian
members to visit the Weizmann Institute whilst overseas on study leave or attending
conferences .

             Further information concerning the Association and the Weizmann Institute
of Science, and membership application forms, are available from the Secretary,
Dr . A .W . Burgess, Walter & Eliza Hall Institute of Medical Research, P .O ., Royal
Melbourne Hospital, Vic . 3050 .
